# Style

Voice for posts (and later the newsletter).

## Reader

A GiveWell-type reader: careful, quantitative, skeptical of hype.

## Rules (from the blueprint)

- Specific numbers, not adjectives.
- State the uncertainty (standard errors / confidence intervals, number of studies,
  heterogeneity).
- No hype.
- Flag weak evidence or questionable extractions plainly.
